Tiers & limits

What each tier is allowed to do. These settings are tier-wide: saving a tier updates every plan of that tier at once, so a 30-day and a 60-day Premium plan can never disagree. Plan-specific settings (name, price, length, on/off) stay on the Plans page. Saving here never changes prices, subscriptions or payments.

How tiers combine. Everyone has the Free tier; a paid plan adds to it and the more generous value wins. So a paid tier can never be lower than Free for a limit or capability, and a capability you switch on for Free is on for everyone. Whatever you set, a profile owner's own privacy choice (hide age / gender / city) always wins, and the per-second anti-abuse limits stay on even for Unlimited.
